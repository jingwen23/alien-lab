Lab Life.

9 Dec 2025

Literature Review Workshop

Led by Dr. Ooi Wee Liam and Dr. Mah Sue Lynn, the workshop held on 9 December covered identifying literature gaps and developing clear literature reviews through practical activities and feedback. Overall, it successfully supported both final-year and second-year students in preparing for their research projects.

11 Nov 2025

PsychoPy Workshop

The workshop that was held on 11 November primarily focus on introducing students to the basics of creating behavioural experiments using PsychoPy. This three-hour hands on session helped guide participants in developing a Numerical Stroop Task while learning about routines, loops, and condition files.

10 Nov 2025

Visit to University of Nottingham Malaysia

The visit on 10 November includes a lab tour and an introduction to the GazePoint GP3 eye tracker, as well as facilities such as the Faraday Cage and EEG equipment. Overall, giving members exposure to advanced research tools and explore opportunities for future collaboration between the instituitions.

7 to 12 Sept 2025

Visit to Mapúa University, Philippines

Led by Dr. Mah Sue Lynn and Dr. Nancy Ng, the visit included masterclasses and workshops on PsychoPy and real-time body tracking using MediaPipe, alongside campus and facility tours and meetings with faculty. The trip also exposed participants to diverse psychology career pathways and potential future collaborations between institutions.
